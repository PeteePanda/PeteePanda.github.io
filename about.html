About me

I'm a Software Developer at Naval Surface Warfare Center, Carderock to maintain and develop their physics based maneuvering and control simulation software.

I grew up in Clarksville, Maryland, with my 2 older sisters and surrounded by pets. Growing up I played a variety video games and had a love for math, so naturally, I knew I wanted a career around computer science. It would allow me to learn more about the medium of which I engage in my main hobby, while also allowing me to deepen my knowledge in the mathematical field

After graduating from River Hill High School, I attended the University of Maryland Baltimore County in 2020 as a Computer Science major and a Cello Performance minor in 2020. While studying computer science, I found a love for Computer Graphics. It tickled my love for math while allowing for fun optimization problem solving when programming. I graduated in 2024 with a 3.6 GPA and a dual Computer Science specialization in data science and machine learning.

Now I live in Germantown, Maryland, with 2 cats of my own, Spoon and Bucket, while we wait for my girlfriend to complete her Doctorate in Physical Therapy.
